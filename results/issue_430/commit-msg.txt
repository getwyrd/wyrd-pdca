fix(430): verify a fragment's full identity, not its chunk id alone

A read of an erasure-coded object could silently return WRONG bytes: the
shared read/repair validation admitted any fragment whose header named the
requested chunk, so a store that served a validly-encoded fragment of the
same chunk at a different index — or one written under a different erasure
scheme — passed the check, and its payload was fed to the decoder under the
requested index. That is wrong reconstruction input, returned with no error
and no repair recorded. Every backend except chunkstore-fs (which verifies
chunk AND index itself) was exposed: the never-wrong-bytes assurance sat in
one backend's goodwill instead of in the shared core code.

A fragment is now admitted into any read, reconstruction, scrub, or
rebalance path only when its decoded header proves the full identity the
committed chunk map requested: the chunk id, the fragment index, and (for
Reed-Solomon) the exact k/m stripe geometry of the committed scheme. A
fragment that fails the check is excluded from the decoder — the read reads
around it when enough intact fragments remain, or fails with a typed error
rather than ever returning wrong bytes — and the affected chunk is enqueued
on the shared repair queue for reconstruction, exactly as a misplaced
fragment already was.
