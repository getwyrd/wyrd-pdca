core: keep a part upload's leases alive while it is being staged

Staging a multipart part writes leases on its slot and on each chunk's owned
entry, but until now only at the reserve and at each chunk's intent. A part
upload slower than one lease TTL, before its first chunk or between chunks,
stopped with a lapse error. Until it stopped, the reaper and the idle fence
could read it as abandoned.

Staging now renews those leases for the whole request:

- One batch moves the slot lease and every owned lease forward together,
  half a TTL ahead, under the session, slot and entry preconditions. The
  slot rewrite in that batch is what keeps a reaper that read the old lease
  as lapsed from fencing a live request.
- The renewal runs while a body read or a fragment write is still pending,
  and is raced against the write deadline and the body-idle bound, so it
  never holds staging past either. A renewal never resets the idle bound.
- A lapsed lease is refused, never revived. Every round re-reads the clock
  and re-checks every lease first. An unknown commit outcome is settled by
  re-reading the slot's exact bytes or reported in doubt, and the loop has
  a fixed round bound.
- A renewal that fails or is refused beside pending fragment writes stops
  staging with those writes accounted for: a write left pending is reported
  as of unknown effect, with the renewal's cause attached, never as a clean
  error or a plain NoSuchUpload while that write may still publish.
- StagedAttempt::renew gives the part commit its last renewal. It takes the
  caller's clock and a coverage the leases must still have after its last
  commit returns, and refuses if it cannot reach it within its rounds.
- The knob clamps and the staging request check refuse a chunk bound whose
  full renewal batch (3 + 2 x chunks operations) would pass MAX_BATCH_OPS.

There is still no production caller; the part commit uses the quiesce
renewal in a later change.
