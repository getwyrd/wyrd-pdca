custodian: run the multipart reaper on every startup shape

Commit 427e5ec8a1f2085d798a61104c318d39d1a9973c added the multipart
reaper, which clears out uploads whose client went silent. The deployed
custodian role only reached it with a reachable D-server fleet. Two
startup shapes never ran it at all:

- With no --endpoints, the role printed "nothing to reconstruct" and
  exited. Abandoned uploads stayed open and their parts stayed on disk.
- With --endpoints given but none reachable, the role panicked before
  its first pass, so a deployment whose fleet is down at every restart
  never reaped anything.

Now the no-endpoints role stays up and runs the reaper every
--interval-secs until Ctrl-C, through the same interval driver the
fleet role uses. A Ctrl-C that arrives during a pass lets the pass
finish, and a stop that is ready when the interval ends wins over the
next pass. On redb the role holds the data-dir lock while it runs, and
it refuses a data dir with no store rather than creating one. It also
refuses --ids, --failure-domains, --gc-expired-pending and
--interval-secs 0, which have no meaning without a fleet.

The empty-fleet role runs exactly one reaper pass, then panics with
the same message and exit status as before. It skips that pass under
--reconcile-after-restore, so a failed restore leaves the store as it
found it.

With a fleet, whole or degraded, behaviour is unchanged: the reaper
already ran outside GC's whole-fleet gate. A new test pins that.

Fixes #1016
