## Invariants to hold (from the design corpus)

- **ADR-0016 dependency rule:** a concrete backend crate depends on `traits` (+ its own backend crate + runtime) and never on `core` or a sibling concrete. `crates/metadata-redb/Cargo.toml` is the shape to mirror.
- **Unchanged trait:** `crates/traits/src/lib.rs:338-351` is byte-for-byte untouched — the whole point of M4 (proposal 0007 §"Graduation criteria").
- **One suite, two backends:** the conformance assertions are shared, not forked — a fork would let the two backends' contracts drift, defeating the "trait pinned by both implementations" goal.
- **Gate honesty:** `cargo xtask ci` remains the single gate and must pass without a live TiKV; the TiKV-backed run is additive (endpoint-gated), reached in CI via the `deploy/` throwaway (ADR-0009 forbids containerizing DST — this is a Tier-1-style complement, not a DST substrate).
