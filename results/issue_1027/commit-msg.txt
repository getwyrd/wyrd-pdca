core: commit, replace or refuse an uploaded multipart part

Staging can store a part's data, but nothing can yet turn it into a part
that a later Complete could use. Multipart upload therefore cannot be
finished, and space held by a failed attempt stays held.

Add the commit half of UploadPart to wyrd-core. A part now commits in one
atomic write. Uploading the same part number again replaces the old part and
schedules its data for deletion in that same write. A part that is too large,
or that would take the upload past its total limit, is refused. An attempt
that stops before its commit is sent gives back its in-flight slot and
schedules its own data for deletion, so repeated failures cannot use up the
upload's slots.

If the store answers a sent commit with a conflict or an unknown result, the
attempt stops safely and sends nothing more. Follow-up changes (#1028, #1029,
#1030) replace those stops. Nothing calls this code in production until the
S3 UploadPart verb is wired (#669), so clients see no change yet.
