core: publish a completed multipart upload past one flat map

Until now completing a multipart upload whose parts add up to more than
MAX_MAP_CHUNKS (158) chunks was refused, so no object that large could be
stored by multipart. complete_upload now publishes such a list segmented: the
staged-publication committer writes the chunk list as seg: records under the
session's segment group at the fence epoch, then the root flip names them.

Each segment batch also moves the session's segments_written cursor, so a
later round of the same attempt resumes from the cursor and never writes a
segment twice. The segment count the flip requires comes from the committer,
not from re-dividing the chunk count.

A fence that wrote no segment is still released with its attempt given back.
A fence whose segment batch landed stays charged against attempts, and its
release installs a retire:records: obligation naming that attempt's segment
group. So every seg: record is named by the published root or by an
obligation, and MAX_COMPLETE_ATTEMPTS bounds how many segment generations one
session can leave behind.

An unknown outcome in a round that had segment batches to send returns a new
in-doubt error, DoubtfulCommit::Publication, and nothing more is sent.

Nothing calls this in production yet; the S3 verb comes with #670. A published
segmented object cannot be deleted or overwritten until the retirement drain
lands (#659).
