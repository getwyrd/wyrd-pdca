multipart: validate sidx: staging entries, guard pending: readers

Multipart upload support needs a per-chunk "staging" record (the
sidx: key space) that tracks which upload owns a chunk and where its
erasure-coded fragments are planned to land. That value shares its
on-disk shape with the plain write lease already stored under
pending:, and until now nothing stopped the two from being confused:
a staging record misfiled under pending: would have its ownership
silently erased on the next lease renewal, or would be reclaimed by
an expiry sweep as if it were an abandoned write.

This adds the staging entry itself (StagedPlacement, OwnedEntry,
decode_owned_entry) with a public, checked way for other crates to
build one, and closes the gap on the pending: side: every reader
(lease renewal, live-lease guards, and both maintenance sweeps) now
goes through one decode entry point per namespace, so a record can
never be read as the wrong shape. A record that still can't be read
(torn, misfiled, or corrupt) is skipped rather than reclaimed or
silently rewritten, and the sweep still finishes the rest of its
work. An untrusted or invalid erasure-coding geometry is rejected as
a typed error instead of decoding into a value that could later
panic.

None of this changes what a plain write lease looks like on disk:
every record written by today's code decodes and re-encodes
byte-for-byte identically, and no sidx: writer exists yet, so the
change is inert until a future multipart-upload feature starts using
it.

Fixes #772
