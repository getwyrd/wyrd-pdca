custodian: rebuild a staged part's chunk under the session fence

A committed part's chunk in an in-progress (`Open`) multipart upload
that lost a fragment stayed short forever unless the client happened
to finish the upload: reconstruction recognised the chunk and kept
its repair obligation queued, but nothing rebuilt or re-placed the
missing fragment (`reconstruction.rs:736-739`, `Assessment::Staged`).

Add `reconstruction::staged`: the same gather/verify path the
committed repair uses, then a destination pre-mark (durable before
any write), a write deadline derived from the pre-mark's own stamp
plus the configured window (never the pass-start time), and a single
adoption compare-and-swap pinned to the upload's session state, the
prior part bytes, the pre-mark, and the destination's drain status.
Any loss at any step leaves the obligation queued and the pre-mark
standing; nothing is ever left written but unevidenced. Destination
writes for a multi-fragment move are sent together so one write's
latency cannot exhaust the next write's authorization window.

A part whose upload has since left `Open`, one named only by an
in-flight (not yet committed) staging entry, or one whose staged
record cannot be trusted whole is left kept and queued, not rebuilt,
same as before.

Regression coverage: `crates/custodian/tests/staged_repair.rs`
(new) exercises the rebuild, the fence-loss legs, the pre-mark and
deadline rules, the adoption's compare-and-swap preconditions, and
the kept-not-rebuilt cases; `crates/dst/tests/custodian.rs` adds a
seeded property sweeping the session fence across every point of the
re-place under `cargo xtask ci`'s madsim run.

Fixes #814
